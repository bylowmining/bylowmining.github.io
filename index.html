For IT and teachers

How this website works

This is a static HTML/CSS/JavaScript site. No student accounts, analytics, advertisements, cookies, third-party scripts, APIs, or server-side database are used. Math questions are generated in the browser. Progress is saved using browser localStorage when permitted.

Important: There is no automatic connection to the teacher dashboard, no access to its stored classroom data, and no automatic Coin payouts. Student reports are self-generated and are not tamper-proof. The teacher reviews and approves rewards.

IT test: Open the website in Safari, enter DEMO, solve a problem, close and reopen the page, and check whether progress persists. Also test exporting a report to Files or Schoolwork.
